Privacy Policy for Roadmap Cafe

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Roadmap Cafe ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

  • Local-First Architecture: All your study plans, checklists, habits, and application progress are stored locally on your own device by default.
  • Account Data & Cloud Sync: If you sign in via Google OAuth or Email, we use secure cloud database services to authenticate your identity and sync your study progress across your personal devices.
  • Personal Information: We only collect basic profile information (such as your email address and account ID provided by Google Authentication) strictly for identity verification and account synchronization.

2. Data Protection & Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or study records with any third parties.
  • We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data brokers.

3. Data Control & Deletion

You can clear your local data anytime from the app settings, or delete your cloud sync records by signing out and requesting account data removal.
