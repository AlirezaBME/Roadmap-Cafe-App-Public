Roadmap Cafe

A quiet, offline-first personal study planner, habit tracker, and immigration roadmap manager.

🎯 Purpose of the Application

Roadmap Cafe is an all-in-one productivity platform designed for students, researchers, and applicants planning their academic journey or immigration roadmap. It provides a serene, focus-oriented environment featuring customizable study boards, milestone tracking, and ambient lo-fi soundscapes.

✨ Key Features

  • Roadmap & Milestone Tracking: Interactive Kanban boards, milestone management, and progress analytics tailored for application deadlines.
  • Cozy Lofi Soundscapes: Built-in ambient audio player featuring soundscapes (rain, thunder, fireplace) and lo-fi stream playback.
  • Offline-First & Private: All your daily tasks, notes, and local timeline records remain safely stored on your device.
  • Cross-Device Cloud Sync: Optional cloud synchronization to keep your roadmaps and task status synced seamlessly across all your devices.

🔒 Use of Google Sign-In & Authentication

Roadmap Cafe offers optional Google Sign-In solely to authenticate users and enable secure cloud synchronization of their personal roadmaps, task lists, and settings across devices.

Data Privacy Commitment: We respect your privacy. Roadmap Cafe only accesses basic user profile information (email address and account ID) provided by Google OAuth for identity verification. We do not access, share, or sell any user data to third parties.
