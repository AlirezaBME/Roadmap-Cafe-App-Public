# Privacy Policy for Roadmap Cafe

**Effective Date:** July 26, 2026

Roadmap Cafe ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **Local-First Architecture:** All your study plans, checklists, habits, and application progress are stored locally on your own device.
- **Account Data & Cloud Sync:** If you sign in via Google OAuth or Email, we use Supabase to securely authenticate your identity and sync your study progress across your personal devices.
- **Personal Information:** We only collect your email address and basic profile information provided by Google Authentication strictly for identity verification and account synchronization.

## 2. Data Protection & Sharing
- We **do not sell, rent, or share** your personal data or study records with any third parties.
- We do not use third-party analytics trackers or advertisement frameworks.

## 3. Data Deletion
You can clear your local data anytime from the app settings, or delete your cloud sync records by signing out and requesting account removal.
