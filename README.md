# ☕ Roadmap Cafe — Immigration & Study Planner

A cozy, offline-first personal study planner, habit tracker, and immigration milestone manager with realistic lofi ambient soundscapes and automated cloud synchronization.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🌧️ **HD Ambient Soundscape & Lofi Radio:** Mix 5 high-definition ambient tracks (Rain on Window, Thunder, Winter Wind, Fireplace Crackle, Cozy Cafe) with 24/7 direct Lofi streams.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Immigration & Study Milestones:** Kanban board, application tracker, deadline notifications, and progress analytics.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First & Offline Ready:** All personal study data remains stored locally on your device.
- ☁️ **Seamless Cloud Auto-Sync:** Optional Supabase & Google Auth integration to sync your study progress automatically across devices.
- 📱 **Cross-Platform Responsive UI:** Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile support.
